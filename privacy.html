Privacy Policy

How Nolubz Network collects, uses, and protects your personal information. Plain, clear, and honest.

Last updated: July 2026
⚠️ Important — Please Read First

Nolubz Network is not a bank, financial institution, money services business, payment processor, or licensed financial intermediary. Nolubz is a Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) ledger-keeping platform. We do not hold, custody, store, or control user funds at any point. All actual money movement is handled exclusively by our regulated, licensed third-party payment partners.

01

Who We Are

Nolubz Network ("Nolubz", "we", "us", or "our") operates the Nolubz platform — a global financial tracking and monitoring application available on Android, iOS, and web. For questions contact us at support@nolubz.com.

02

What Nolubz Is — and Is Not

Nolubz is a No-Deposit Holding SaaS platform. We do not accept, hold, store, receive, transmit, or control your money at any time. The NOLZ balance is a ledger representation of transaction activity facilitated by third-party licensed payment providers.

What Nolubz does:

  • Provides a unified interface to connect with licensed payment providers
  • Records and displays your transaction history in a clear, organised ledger
  • Facilitates communication between you and our licensed payment partners
  • Provides savings goal tracking, escrow coordination, and bill payment facilitation

What Nolubz does not do:

  • Hold, custody, or store your money
  • Act as a bank or licensed financial institution
  • Provide financial advice, investment services, or credit facilities
  • Issue electronic money or payment instruments
03

Information We Collect

  • Full name, email address, country, phone number at registration
  • Identity verification documents (for KYC — processed by our KYC partners)
  • Transaction records — amounts, dates, types, and references
  • Device information, IP address, and app usage data
  • Push notification tokens for transactional alerts
04

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your Nolubz account
  • To display your transaction history and ledger balance
  • To facilitate connections between you and licensed payment providers
  • To send transactional notifications about your account activity
  • To verify your identity as required by KYC requirements
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud or unauthorised access
  • To comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations
05

What We Never Do With Your Data

  • We will never sell your personal data to any third party
  • We will never share your data with advertisers for marketing purposes
  • We will never use your transaction data to build advertising profiles
  • We will never share your identity documents beyond our KYC verification partners
06

Third-Party Payment Partners

When you use Nolubz to deposit, withdraw, or transfer funds, your transaction is processed by licensed third-party payment partners including Transak, MoonPay, Kotani Pay, Monnify, Paystack, Stripe, TransFi, and Crossmint. We share only the minimum data necessary to facilitate your requested transaction.

07

Data Security

  • All data transmitted is encrypted using TLS
  • Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256
  • All transactions require PIN authentication before processing
  • Access to user data is strictly limited on a need-to-know basis
08

Your Rights

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data
  • Portability — request your data in a machine-readable format

Contact us at support@nolubz.com. We respond within 30 days.

09

Children's Privacy

Nolubz is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you through the Nolubz app and update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Questions about this policy?

We are committed to transparency. If anything in this policy is unclear, reach out — we will respond within 48 hours.
